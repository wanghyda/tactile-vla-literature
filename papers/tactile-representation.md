# Tactile Representation and Sensing

## Sparsh

**Sparsh: Self-supervised Touch Representations for Vision-based Tactile Sensing**
Carolina Higuera et al. 2024, conference.
[arXiv:2410.24090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24090) · [Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sparsh) · [Project](https://sparsh-ssl.github.io/)

Self-supervised tactile representation learning across vision-based tactile sensors.

## T3

**Transferable Tactile Transformers for Representation Learning Across Diverse Sensors and Tasks**
Jialiang Zhao, Yuxiang Ma, Lirui Wang, Edward H. Adelson. 2024, preprint.
[arXiv:2406.13640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13640) · [Code](https://github.com/alanzjl/t3)

A tactile transformer trained for transfer across sensors and tasks.

## AnyTouch

**AnyTouch: Learning Unified Static-Dynamic Representation across Multiple Visuo-tactile Sensors**
Ruoxuan Feng et al. 2025, preprint.
[arXiv:2502.12191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12191) · [Project](https://gewu-lab.github.io/AnyTouch/)

Unified static and dynamic representations are learned across multiple optical tactile sensors.

## UniTouch

**UniTouch: A Unified Representation of Touch and Vision for Robotic Manipulation**
Fengyu Yang et al. 2024, conference.
[arXiv:2401.18084](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.18084)

Aligns touch and vision representations for robotic manipulation.

## DIGIT

**DIGIT: A Novel Design for a Low-Cost Compact High-Resolution Tactile Sensor with Application to In-Hand Manipulation**
Mike Lambeta et al. 2020, journal.
[arXiv:2005.14679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14679) · [Code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/digit)

A compact optical tactile sensor for high-resolution contact imaging and in-hand manipulation.

## GelSight

**GelSight: High-Resolution Robot Tactile Sensors for Estimating Geometry and Force**
Wenzhen Yuan, Siyuan Dong, Edward H. Adelson. 2017, journal.
[Publisher](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/17/12/2762) · [DOI:10.3390/s17122762](https://doi.org/10.3390/s17122762)

An optical tactile sensor for contact geometry and force estimation.

## GelTip

**GelTip: A Finger-shaped Optical Tactile Sensor for Robotic Manipulation**
Daniel Fernandes Gomes, Zhonglin Lin, Shan Luo. 2020, preprint.
[arXiv:2008.05404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.05404)

A finger-shaped optical tactile sensor with contact coverage around the fingertip.

## TacTip

**Soft Biomimetic Optical Tactile Sensing with the TacTip: A Review**
Nathan F. Lepora. 2021, review.
[arXiv:2105.14455](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.14455) · [Project](https://tactip.org/)

A review of soft biomimetic optical tactile sensors in robot contact sensing.
