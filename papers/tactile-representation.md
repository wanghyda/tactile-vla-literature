# 触觉表征、基础模型与传感器

## Sparsh

- **Sparsh: Self-supervised Touch Representations for Vision-based Tactile Sensing**
- Carolina Higuera et al. (2024, CoRL)
- [arXiv:2410.24090](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24090) · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sparsh) · [project](https://sparsh-ssl.github.io/)
- 在 DIGIT、GelSight 等视觉触觉传感器上进行自监督表征学习，并提供 TacBench。
- **阅读评价：** 触觉编码器和基准的首选基础工作。

## T3

- **Transferable Tactile Transformers for Representation Learning Across Diverse Sensors and Tasks**
- Jialiang Zhao, Yuxiang Ma, Lirui Wang, Edward H. Adelson (2024, arXiv preprint)
- [arXiv:2406.13640](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13640) · [code](https://github.com/alanzjl/t3)
- 基于 Foundation Tactile 数据集学习跨传感器、跨任务可迁移表示。

## AnyTouch

- **AnyTouch: Learning Unified Static-Dynamic Representation across Multiple Visuo-tactile Sensors**
- Ruoxuan Feng et al. (2025, arXiv preprint)
- [arXiv:2502.12191](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12191) · [project](https://gewu-lab.github.io/AnyTouch/)
- 学习跨光学触觉传感器的静态与动态统一表示。

## UniTouch

- **UniTouch: A Unified Representation of Touch and Vision for Robotic Manipulation**
- Fengyu Yang et al. (2024, CVPR)
- [arXiv:2401.18084](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.18084)
- 将触觉嵌入与视觉、语言和音频空间对齐，为多模态触觉策略提供统一表示。

## DIGIT

- **DIGIT: A Novel Design for a Low-Cost Compact High-Resolution Tactile Sensor with Application to In-Hand Manipulation**
- Mike Lambeta et al. (2020, RA-L)
- [arXiv:2005.14679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.14679) · [code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/digit)
- 低成本、高分辨率视觉触觉指尖传感器；大量学习型触觉工作采用该平台。
