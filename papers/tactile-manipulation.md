# Tactile Manipulation and Control

## DexTouch

**DexTouch: Learning to Seek and Manipulate Objects with Tactile Dexterity**
Kang-Won Lee, Yuzhe Qin, Xiaolong Wang, Soo-Chul Lim. 2024, preprint.
[arXiv:2401.12496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.12496) · [Project](https://lee-kangwon.github.io/dextouch/)

Tactile-only object seeking and manipulation with a multi-fingered robot.

## Learning Tactile Insertion in the Real World

**Learning Tactile Insertion in the Real World**
Daniel Palenicek et al. 2024, preprint.
[arXiv:2405.00383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00383)

Real-world insertion learning with tactile feedback and model-based reinforcement learning.

## DexiTac

**DexiTac: Tactile-Driven Dexterous Manipulation with a Soft Reconfigurable Gripper**
Chenghua Lu et al. 2024, preprint.
[arXiv:2405.02897](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.02897)

Tactile-driven grasp stability detection and manipulation with a reconfigurable gripper.

## Tac2Motion

**Tac2Motion: Tactile-Based Reward Shaping for Dexterous Manipulation**
Yitaek Kim, Casper Hewson Rask, Christoffer Sloth. 2025, preprint.
[arXiv:2509.17812](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.17812)

Contact and tactile signals are used for reward shaping in dexterous manipulation.

## The Feeling of Success

**The Feeling of Success: Does Touch Sensing Help Predict Grasp Outcomes?**
2017, conference.
[arXiv:1710.05512](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.05512)

Visuo-tactile grasp outcome prediction from large-scale robot grasping data.

## More Than a Feeling

**More Than a Feeling: Learning to Grasp via Visual and Tactile Sensing**
2018, conference.
[arXiv:1805.11085](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.11085)

End-to-end grasping with visual and tactile observations.

## Evetac

**Evetac: An Event-based Optical Tactile Sensor for High-speed Robotic Manipulation**
2024, journal.
[arXiv:2312.01236](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.01236)

Event-based optical tactile sensing for high-rate contact and slip-related control.

## Reactive Diffusion Policy

**Reactive Diffusion Policy: Slow-Fast Visual-Tactile Policy Learning for Contact-Rich Manipulation**
2025, preprint.
[arXiv:2503.02881](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02881)

A slow visual policy and fast tactile feedback policy are combined for contact-rich control.
