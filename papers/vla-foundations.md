# Robot Foundation Models and VLA

## RT-1

**RT-1: Robotics Transformer for Real-World Control at Scale**
Anthony Brohan et al. 2022, conference.
[arXiv:2212.06817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06817) · [Project](https://robotics-transformer1.github.io/)

A transformer policy for language-conditioned real-world robot control.

## RT-2

**RT-2: Vision-Language-Action Models Transfer Web Knowledge to Robotic Control**
Brianna Zitkovich et al. 2023, conference.
[arXiv:2307.15818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.15818) · [Proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v229/zitkovich23a.html)

Transfers vision-language model knowledge to robot actions represented as tokens.

## OpenVLA

**OpenVLA: An Open-Source Vision-Language-Action Model**
Moo Jin Kim et al. 2024, conference.
[arXiv:2406.09246](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09246) · [Code](https://github.com/openvla/openvla)

An open 7B vision-language-action model and training framework for robot manipulation.

## Octo

**Octo: An Open-Source Generalist Robot Policy**
Octo Model Team et al. 2024, conference.
[arXiv:2405.12213](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12213) · [Code](https://github.com/octo-models/octo)

An open generalist robot policy trained on multi-robot demonstrations.

## π₀

**π₀: A Vision-Language-Action Flow Model for General Robot Control**
Kevin Black et al. 2024, preprint.
[arXiv:2410.24164](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.24164) · [Project](https://www.physicalintelligence.company/blog/pi0)

A flow-matching vision-language-action model for continuous robot control.

## Diffusion Policy

**Diffusion Policy: Visuomotor Policy Learning via Action Diffusion**
Cheng Chi et al. 2023, conference.
[arXiv:2303.04137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04137)

Diffusion-based action generation for visuomotor robot policies.

## ACT

**Learning Fine-Grained Bimanual Manipulation with Low-Cost Hardware**
Tony Z. Zhao et al. 2023, conference.
[arXiv:2304.13705](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.13705)

Action chunking and temporal aggregation for bimanual manipulation.

## BridgeData V2

**BridgeData V2: A Dataset for Robot Learning at Scale**
Michael Ahn et al. 2023, conference.
[arXiv:2308.12952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.12952)

A multi-task robot manipulation dataset collected across environments and embodiments.

## Open X-Embodiment

**Open X-Embodiment: Robotic Learning Datasets and RT-X Models**
Open X-Embodiment Collaboration. 2023, preprint.
[arXiv:2310.08864](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08864) · [Project](https://robotics-transformer-x.github.io/)

A large multi-robot dataset and RT-X model family for cross-embodiment learning.
